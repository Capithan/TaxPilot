# TaxPilot Demo Video - Narration Script

**Duration:** ~2 minutes  
**Tone:** Professional, direct, focused

---

## 2-MINUTE VERSION (Recommended)

### OPENING (0:00 - 0:10)

> "This is TaxPilot — an AI assistant that handles client intake, document checklists, and appointment scheduling in one guided conversation."

---

### INTAKE FLOW (0:10 - 0:45)

*[Show: Chat with rapid Q&A]*

> "The client answers a series of questions — name, filing status, spouse details, dependents, income sources, and deductions.
>
> The system captures W-2 employment, freelance 1099 work, investments, and identifies special situations like cryptocurrency or foreign accounts."

---

### SUMMARY & CONFIRMATION (0:45 - 1:00)

*[Show: Summary displayed, client confirms]*

> "Everything collected is presented in a summary. The client confirms or requests edits before proceeding."

---

### DOCUMENT CHECKLIST (1:00 - 1:20)

*[Show: Personalized checklist appearing]*

> "Based on their specific situation, TaxPilot generates a personalized document checklist — W-2s, 1099s, crypto records, rental statements — with tips on where to get each document."

---

### TAX PRO MATCHING (1:20 - 1:40)

*[Show: Complexity score and tax pro recommendation]*

> "A complexity score is calculated. The system matches the client with the right specialist — in this case, Michael Chen, who handles cryptocurrency and investments."

---

### APPOINTMENT & CLOSE (1:40 - 2:00)

*[Show: Appointment confirmation, reminders setup]*

> "The appointment is booked, reminders are set, and the client is ready — with all their documents and the right tax pro assigned.
>
> That's TaxPilot. Clients arrive prepared. Appointments run faster."

---

## SCREEN RECORDING TIPS

1. **Type naturally** — Don't rush the typing, let viewers read along
2. **Pause on key screens** — Give 2-3 seconds on summary, checklist, and tax pro match
3. **Use realistic data** — John Smith, married filing jointly, 2 kids, W-2 + freelance + crypto
4. **Show the flow progress** — If visible, highlight progression through stages
5. **Keep chat window centered** — Minimize distractions, focus on the conversation
